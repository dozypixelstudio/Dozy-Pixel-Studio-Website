About Dozy Pixel Studio

An independent studio making game-ready 3D assets, slowly and carefully.

The sleepy sloth on the moon

Our mascot is a sloth dozing on a crescent moon. It is a reminder of how we like to work: without rushing, paying attention to the details, and taking the time to get things right.

We think good assets come from patience. Slow-crafted, so they can be built into your game fast.

What we make

We start with 3D props and environments for game developers. VFX will follow later.

Our art style: Stylized

How we work

Every asset is made to be easy to pick up and use: tidy models, sensible textures and clear notes on what is included.

  • modeling tool - Blender
  • texturing tool - Substance
  • engine you test in - Unity

An honest indie studio

Dozy Pixel Studio is independent and small. We would rather tell you plainly what an asset includes than oversell it. If something is unclear, ask us.
