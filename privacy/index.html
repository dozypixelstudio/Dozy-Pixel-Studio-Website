Privacy

Short version: we do not track you.

What this site does not do

  • No analytics or tracking scripts.
  • No cookies.
  • No forms. The site collects no data from you.

What we receive

We only receive the emails people choose to send us. We use them to reply and to help with support, and we do not share them for marketing.

Third parties

  • Fonts load from Google Fonts. Google may receive your IP address when that happens, as described in Google's own privacy policy.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Links to Fab, the Unity Asset Store and social profiles lead to other sites with their own privacy policies. Purchases and accounts are handled entirely by those stores.

If we ever add analytics or other services, we will update this page first.
